House rules

Payment

Paid in full when I finish, before I leave. Cash or Zelle. No deposit to book.

Cancelling

Cancel at least 24 hours ahead. A late cancel or no show needs a $25 Zelle deposit to book again.

Bedrooms

I only clean a bedroom its resident has agreed to. Locked or closed bedrooms are skipped and not charged.

Your things

I never touch watches, jewelry, phones, cash, laptops, papers or clothes. I clean around them and photograph every room before and after.

Drawers and closets

Cleaned inside when empty. Ones holding clothes or personal items stay closed and get wiped outside. Kitchen drawers are always fine.

What I skip

Anything unsafe to clean: mold, bodily fluids or pests.
